## Favorite Movies

**Kimi No Na Wa**
*Director*: Makoto Shinkai
*Distributor*: Toho Co., Ltd
*Runtime*: 1h 50m

> Two teenagers share a profound, magical connection upon discovering they are swapping bodies. Things manage to become even more complicated when the boy and girl decide to meet in person.

**The Truman Show**
*Director*: Peter Weir
*Distributor*: Paramount Pictures
*Runtime*: 1h 43m

> He doesn't know it, but everything in Truman Burbank's (Jim Carrey) life is part of a massive TV set. Executive producer Christof (Ed Harris) orchestrates "The Truman Show," a live broadcast of Truman's every move captured by hidden cameras. Cristof tries to control Truman's mind, even removing his true love, Sylvia (Natascha McElhone), from the show and replacing her with Meryl (Laura Linney). As Truman gradually discovers the truth, however, he must decide whether to act on it.

**The Iron Claw**
*Director*: Sean Durkin
*Distributor*: A24, Mozinet
*Runtime*: 2h 12m

> Tells the story of the Von Erich brothers as they make history in the highly competitive world of professional wrestling in the early 1980s. Through tragedy and triumph, the brothers seek larger-than-life immortality on the biggest stage in sports.

**The Fate Series**
*Creator*: Kinoko Nasu, Takashi Takeuchi
*Series Count*: 7 Main TV Series, 5 Prominent Films, 12 Spin-offs

> A vast franchise centered on the Holy Grail War, a deadly magical tournament where mages, known as "Masters," summon legendary heroes from history to serve as "Servants". The last two standing fighters fight for a chance to obtain the Holy Grail, a magical chalice that can grant any wish. The franchise includes numerous installments, each exploring different timelines and characters within this shared universe.

**The Kingsman Trilogy**
*Director*: Matthew Vaughn
*Distributor*: 20th Century Studio
*Runtime*: 6h 41m in total

> Follows the story of the independent intelligence agency Kingsman across different timelines. It begins with the origin story of the agency in The King's Man, where a group forms to prevent World War I. The main saga then continues with the modern-day adventures of a street-smart recruit, Eggsy, and his mentor Harry Hart in Kingsman: The Secret Service and Kingsman: The Golden Circle, which see them facing global threats from villainous organizations.

**Whiplash**
*Director*: Damien Chazelle
*Distributor*: Sony Pictures
*Runtime*: 1h 47m

> Andrew Neiman (Miles Teller) is an ambitious young jazz drummer, in pursuit of rising to the top of his elite music conservatory. Terence Fletcher (J.K. Simmons), an instructor known for his terrifying teaching methods, discovers Andrew and transfers the aspiring drummer into the top jazz ensemble, forever changing the young man's life. But Andrew's passion to achieve perfection quickly spirals into obsession, as his ruthless teacher pushes him to the brink of his ability and his sanity.

**Spider-Man: Spider-verse Franchise**
*Director*: Bob Persichetti, Peter Ramsey, Rodney Rothman, Joaquim Dos Santos, Kemp Powers, and Justin K. Thompson
*Distributor*: Sony Pictures
*Runtime*: 4hr 17m in total

> Bitten by a radioactive spider in the subway, Brooklyn teenager Miles Morales suddenly develops mysterious powers that transform him into the one and only Spider-Man. When he meets Peter Parker, he soon realizes that there are many others who share his special, high-flying talents. Miles must now use his newfound skills to battle the evil Kingpin, a hulking madman who can open portals to other universes and pull different versions of Spider-Man into our world.
